# Mechanism before claim

- Pattern ID: `PAT-NARR-001`
- Problem: a value claim feels generic or unsupported.
- Mechanism: show the product action and resulting state before or while stating the benefit.
- Use for: feature launches and warm-audience product demos.
- Avoid when: the audience needs category/problem context before understanding the interface.
- Non-copy rule: derive the beat order from client evidence, not a source video's scene sequence.
