# Competitor intelligence

Create `artifacts/competitor-intelligence.json`. For each competitor register sources and capture:

- offer, target customer, named pain, positioning, proof, and CTA;
- what the product video shows, in what order, and with what visual mechanism;
- observations separately from interpretations.

Synthesize recurring messages, table-stakes claims, overused visual devices, under-explained mechanisms, and potential opportunity gaps. An opportunity is a strategic hypothesis unless the client's own evidence confirms it. Never convert competitor messaging into evidence about the client's customers or copy a competitor's expression.
