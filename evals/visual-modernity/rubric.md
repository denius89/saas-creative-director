# Visual modernity and production usability rubric

Score each dimension from 1 to 5 after viewing anonymized outputs at the same scale and duration. Reviewers do not see the generating version, prompt, reference source, or claimed trend label.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product truth | invented or misleading state | plausible with disclosed simplification | faithful mechanism and evidence |
| Communication hierarchy | no stable focal path | main point understandable | attention sequence is immediate and intentional |
| Visual causality | decorative sequence | some actions connect to results | every major claim has a legible mechanism or proof |
| Contemporary relevance | visibly templated or dated | competent but generic | current without depending on a superficial trend |
| Sequence rhythm | repetitive or rushed | workable pacing | holds, escalation, and transitions support comprehension |
| Reference originality | recognizable imitation | partly abstracted | source principle is transformed into client-specific grammar |
| Production clarity | animation must be invented later | most states and notes present | states, events, anchors, timing, locked/free split are actionable |
| Figma editability | flattened or unstable | mostly editable | native, named, idempotent, and safe to revise |

Blocking failures override the total: fabricated claim, undisclosed product simplification, copied expression, unreadable critical evidence, stale approval, duplicate import, destructive overwrite of human edits, or a falsely editable raster.

The modernity score never substitutes for product truth or production usability. Report dimensions independently and preserve reviewer comments tied to scene and object IDs.
